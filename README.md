# Buzzword Bubbles

Pop the corporate buzzwords before they float away. Don't pop the sea friends.

Play it here

# How to play
• Buzzword bubbles rise from the bottom. Tap or swipe to pop them.
• Let one float off the top and you lose a life.
• Bubbles carrying a sea friend (pufferfish, octopus, crab, jellyfish, starfish) are off limits. Pop one and you lose a life.
• Every 10 pops in a row raises your multiplier, up to x5.
• The glowing pearl clears every buzzword on screen.
• You have 3 lives, and the game gets faster every few seconds.

# About

A single index.html file. No backend, no libraries, no tracking. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.
